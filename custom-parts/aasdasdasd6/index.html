    This project involved manufacturing precision brass fittings for an industrial equipment manufacturer. The client required high-quality threaded components with excellent surface finish for their hydraulic systems.

    Client Requirements

    Requirement Specification
    Material Brass C36000 (free-machining)
    Tolerances ±0.015mm
    Surface Finish Ra 1.6μm
    Quantity 1000 units
    Delivery 12 days

    Challenge

    The client needed:

    • High-quality threaded connections
    • Consistent dimensions for assembly
    • Excellent surface finish for aesthetics
    • Reliable sealing surfaces

    Solution

    Manufacturing Process

    1. Material Selection

      • Brass C36000 for excellent machinability
      • Certified material with traceability

      • Final cleaning
