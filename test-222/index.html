    Precision Aluminum CNC Milling for Automotive Components

    Project Overview

    This project involved manufacturing precision aluminum components for an automotive parts manufacturer. The client required high-quality parts with tight tolerances for their engine assembly line.

    Client Requirements

    Requirement Specification
    Material Aluminum 6061-T6
    Tolerances ±0.01mm
    Surface Finish Ra 0.8μm
    Quantity 500 units
    Delivery 10 days

    Challenge

    The client needed:

    • Complex geometries with tight tolerances
    • Excellent surface finish for assembly
    • Consistent quality across all 500 units
    • Fast delivery for production schedule

    Solution

    Manufacturing Process

    1. Material Selection

      • Aluminum 6061-T6 for strength and machinability
      • Certified material with full traceability

    2. CNC Programming

      • 4-axis milling for complex features
      • Optimized toolpaths for efficiency
      • Fixture design for repeatability

    3. Machining

      • 4-axis CNC milling
      • Premium carbide tooling
      • Coolant optimization

    4. Quality Control

      • In-process inspection
      • CMM measurement
      • 100% final inspection

    5. Surface Finishing

      • Bead blasting for uniform finish
      • Type II anodizing for durability

    Inspection 100% passed
    Delivery 10 days
    Quality 99.9% satisfaction
