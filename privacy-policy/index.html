    Privacy Policy

    Last Updated: January 2025

    Introduction

    Kinsai Machining (“we,” “our,” or “us”) is committed to protecting your privacy and the confidentiality of your intellectual property. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you use our services.

    Information We Collect

    Personal Information

    • Name and contact details
    • Email address
    • Phone number
    • Company name
    • Shipping address

    Technical Information

    • Design files and drawings
    • CAD models (STEP, IGES, DWG, DXF)
    • Project specifications
    • Quality requirements

    Communication

    • Email correspondence
    • Chat logs
    • Phone call records
    • Quote requests

    How We Use Your Information

    To Provide Services

    • Process your RFQ and orders
    • Provide quotes and DFM analysis
    • Manufacture custom parts
    • Manage shipping and delivery

    To Communicate

    • Respond to inquiries
    • Provide order updates
    • Share technical information
    • Send promotional materials (with consent)

    To Improve

    • Analyze service usage
    • Optimize manufacturing processes
    • Enhance customer experience
    • Develop new capabilities

    Intellectual Property Protection

    NDA Commitment

    We take intellectual property protection extremely seriously:

    • All designs remain your property
    • We never share your designs with third parties
    • Strict NDA signed for every project
    • Secure file storage and transfer

    File Security

    • Encrypted file transfer
    • Password-protected access
    • Secure cloud storage
    • Automated file deletion after project completion

    Data Storage and Security

    Storage

    • Primary Storage: Encrypted databases
    • File Storage: Secure cloud servers
    • Backup: Daily encrypted backups

    Security Measures

    • SSL Encryption for all data transfer
    • Firewall Protection for servers
    • Access Control restricted to authorized personnel
    • Regular Security Audits

    Data Retention

    • Active Projects: Data retained until project completion
    • Completed Projects: Data retained for 2 years
    • Quotes: Data retained for 1 year
    • Contact Information: Retained unless deletion requested

    Information Sharing

    We Do Not Share Your Information

    • With third parties (unless required by law)
    • For marketing purposes (without consent)
    • With competitors
    • To unauthorized personnel

    Limited Sharing (with your consent)

    • Shipping Partners: For delivery only
    • Financial Partners: For payment processing
    • Quality Partners: For certification purposes
