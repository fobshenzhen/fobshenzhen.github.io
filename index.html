About Kinsai Company

5-Axis CNC Milling & Precision Turning Capabilities

Established in 2009, Kinsai Machining has grown from a small CNC shop into a professional China custom CNC parts manufacturer operating in compliance with ISO 9001 standards. We offer complete project solutions – from the first rapid prototype to precision low volume production. With over 15 years of expertise, our engineering team provides reliable one-stop support, ensuring every part meets your exact specifications. "One contact, the same high quality, always on time – just like having your own factory in China."

Technical Capabilities: Our state-of-the-art facility is equipped with advanced 3-axis, 4-axis, and 5-axis CNC milling and CNC turning centers, achieving tight tolerances up to +/- 0.01mm. We specialize in machining a wide range of materials, like billet Aluminum 6061/7075, Brass, Stainless Steel, Titanium, and plastics like PEEK & POM. Surface finishing: Anodizing (Type II/III), bead blasting, PVD plating, brushing, etc.

Global Supply & Trust: Kinsai proudly serves a loyal global customer base, exporting over 80% of our custom machined components to North America, Europe, and Asia. We support your growth with No MOQ (1pcs OK), secure PayPal payments, and DDP (Duties Paid) shipping.
