    How much does CNC machining cost in China?

    How much does CNC machining cost in China?

    One of the most common questions we receive is about CNC machining costs in China. While prices vary based on numerous factors, this comprehensive guide will help you understand the cost structure and estimate your project budget.

    Cost Breakdown Overview

    Typical Cost Components

    Component Percentage Description
    Material 10-25% Raw material cost
    Machining 40-60% Machine time and labor
    Finishing 10-20% Surface treatments
    Quality Control 5-10% Inspection and testing
    Shipping 5-10% Logistics and duties
    Profit 10-15% Manufacturer margin

    Average Cost Ranges

    Part Size Simple (1-2 ops) Complex (5+ ops)
    Small (< 5cm) $3-15 $15-40
    Medium (5-20cm) $10-50 $40-120
    Large (20-50cm) $50-200 $120-500
    Extra Large (>50cm) $200-800 $500-2000+

    Detailed Cost Factors

    1. Material Costs

    Material Price Range (USD/kg) Machinability
    Aluminum 6061 $3-5 Excellent
    Aluminum 7075 $6-10 Good
    Brass $5-8 Excellent
    Stainless Steel 304 $3-6 Fair
    Stainless Steel 316 $5-8 Fair
    Steel 1045 $2-4 Good
    Titanium Gr5 $30-50 Poor
    PEEK $60-100 Fair
    POM (Delrin) $4-7 Excellent

    Material Cost Calculation

    Material Cost = Material Price × Part Volume × Scrap Rate

    Example for Aluminum 6061:

    • Part Volume: 100cm³ (0.1kg)

    • Material Price: $4/kg

    • Scrap Rate: 2x (machining waste)

    • Material Cost = $4 × 0.1 × 2 = $0.80
