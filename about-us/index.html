Precision CNC Machining Manufacturer in China

Your Reliable China Partner for Billet Machined & Precision Turned Components

Dongguan Kinsai Machining Limited is a premier, engineering-driven CNC factory, specializing in precision billet milled and turned components. We provide global innovators with specialized manufacturing solutions, including 3 to 5 axis milling, precision turning, and rich surface finishing. From complex automotive parts, camera accessories, to high-end architectural hardware, we transform complex designs into exquisite metal reality.

As your reliable CNC machining partner in China, we offer more than just machining. Our engineering team provides free DFM (Design for Manufacturing) feedback to optimize your parts and performance from the first prototype to mass production. Think of us as your offshore CNC manufacturing department — dedicated to perfect quality, responsive communication, and 100% on-time delivery.

With 15+ years of specialized CNC expertise, Kinsai has grown into a well-equipped, ISO9001 Compliant factory. We understand that in precision manufacturing, the smallest detail defines the final performance. This detail-first philosophy is embedded in our quality management system, ensuring every billet component meets the most demanding tolerances (±0.01mm) and aesthetic standards. At present, 80%+ of our production is dedicated to the international market, primarily serving engineering partners in North America, Europe, Oceania, etc.

Our machining experience spans diverse high-requirement sectors, including Automotive & Motorsport, Cinema & Photo Gear, Optical Systems, Robotics, Decorative Hardware, etc. We are skilled in working with a wide range of materials, such as aluminum (6061/7075), brass (c360/lead free), stainless steel (304/316), titanium, and engineering plastics (PEEK/POM). Ready to start your new project? Just send us your STEP, IGS, or CAD files for a free DFM review and a quote within 24 hours — let's build a partnership based on quality work and mutual trust.

Our 2500 sqm production facility is located at No.6, Xinhua Rd, Xinhe, Wanjiang District, Dongguan, Guangdong, China. We welcome global clients for on-site audits.
